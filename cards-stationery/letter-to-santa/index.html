In short

  • A good letter to Santa has a greeting, a line about the child's year, a short wish list in order, a question for Santa and a signature.
  • Children who can't write yet can dictate, draw their wishes or cut pictures from a catalogue and glue them flat.
  • 2026 deadlines from the official pages: USPS Operation Santa December 5, Canada Post December 9, the Post Office's Santa service in the UK December 9.
  • Canada Post needs no stamp inside Canada; USPS asks for a First-Class stamp and a return address.
  • USPS removes last names, addresses and school names before a letter is shown to the people who adopt letters.

A letter to Santa needs five parts: "Dear Santa", a line about the child's year, a short wish list in order, a question for Santa, and the child's name. To get a reply, mail it before the 2026 deadline: December 5 for USPS Operation Santa in the US, December 9 for Canada Post (no stamp needed in Canada) and December 9 for the Post Office's Santa service in the UK.

What to write in a letter to Santa

Children freeze at a blank page. A fixed order helps, and it doubles as a fill-in template if you print it with gaps.

  1. Greeting: "Dear Santa," or "Dear Father Christmas,".
  2. Who they are: first name, age, and where they live in words a child would use ("in the blue house near the park").
  3. Their year: one thing they learned, one kind thing they did, one thing they found hard. Santa likes honesty.
  4. A question for Santa: how the reindeer are, what Mrs Claus bakes, whether elves get snow days.
  5. The wish list, best wish first. Two to four items is plenty.
  6. Something for someone else: a wish for a sibling, a grandparent, or a friend.
  7. Sign-off: "Love from" and the first name, plus a drawing.

USPS asks for the gifts in order of preference, specific descriptions (the size, the colour, the exact book title) and requests that aren't too expensive. Those three rules make a better letter wherever you mail it.

Letter to Santa examples by age

Our own samples, to read aloud or copy.

For a child of three or four, dictated to a parent:

Dear Santa, I am four. I can do a forward roll now. Do the reindeer like carrots? I would like a red fire truck and a book about dinosaurs. Please bring Grandma some warm socks. Love from Mia

For a child of six or seven, written with help on spelling:

Dear Santa, my name is Leo and I am seven. This year I learned to ride my bike without stabilisers and I helped my sister tidy her room (twice). What is your favourite cookie? For Christmas I would like the space Lego set with the rocket, a yellow scooter helmet, and a torch for reading in bed. I hope your elves are not too busy. Love from Leo

For an older child who still writes:

Dear Santa, I'm ten, so this might be one of my last letters, but I wanted to write anyway. School got harder this year and I joined the choir. My list is short: a sketchbook with thick paper and a set of drawing pencils. Could you also leave something for our neighbour, who walks our bins out every week? Merry Christmas, Ana

Ideas for children who can't write yet

The letter is the child's, even if an adult holds the pen.

  • Dictation: the child talks, you write their exact words, then they add a scribble signature.
  • Drawing: one box per wish, with you writing a label underneath.
  • Catalogue cut-outs: pictures of toys trimmed and glued on. Use a glue stick and keep it flat (see the USPS note below).
  • Handprint or fingerprint "signature" in washable paint, dried before folding.
  • Tracing: you write the words lightly in pencil, the child goes over them in crayon.

For children learning letters, a printable with dotted lines does the same job as tracing, and the dot marker activities we list work as a warm-up for the youngest.

Where to mail a letter to Santa in 2026

Every address and date below is copied from the official page of each service, read on November 20, 2026.

United States (USPS Operation Santa) Santa, 123 Elf Road, North Pole, 88888 December 5, 2026 First-Class stamp, upper right Full name and address, upper left
Canada (Canada Post) Santa Claus, North Pole, H0H 0H0, Canada December 9, 2026 Not needed inside Canada Required
United Kingdom (Post Office page) Santa/Father Christmas, Santa's Grotto, Reindeerland, XM4 5HQ 9 December 2026 Yes, the page says to use a stamp Name and address, so Santa can reply

A few details from those pages:

  • Canada Post asks families to mail all their children's letters in one envelope, and welcomes drawings and questions. The postal code is H0H 0H0 with zeros.
  • Canada Post says it can't guarantee delivery dates but that Santa responds to all the letters he receives.
  • The UK Post Office page also gives a Welsh address for a reply in Welsh, and a Royal Mail email for braille or BSL replies.
  • USPS Operation Santa is open to people living in the continental United States, Hawaii and Puerto Rico.

Living somewhere else? Look on your own post office's website rather than a third-party "Santa address" page.

What happens to a letter sent to USPS Operation Santa

The US programme works differently from Canada's and the UK's. Letters are posted online, where members of the public adopt them and send gifts. Key dates for 2026 from the USPS site: letters accepted from September 14, adopters registered from November 2, adoption from November 16, last day for letter writers December 5, last day for adopters December 12.

Before a letter goes online, USPS says last names, addresses, and school or community names are blacked out on the letter before it is posted. Even so, keep the letter itself free of phone numbers, email addresses or a photo of the child; there's no reason Santa needs them.

USPS is also clear that nothing is guaranteed: not every letter is uploaded, adopted, or answered. If a reply matters to your child, keep a backup plan, such as a letter "from Santa" written by a grandparent.

Printing and decorating the letter

Printable templates save the blank-page problem. Monday Mandala's PDFs, for example, are sized for US Letter and also fit A4.

What to watch for:

  • Keep it flat. USPS warns that glitter and glued-on items like macaroni and beads can stop a letter being uploaded.
  • Write or print clearly. USPS asks for legible handwriting; for a dictated letter, print the adult's part in a plain font.
  • Choose a template with space for drawing. Younger children spend longer on the picture than on the words.
  • Use ordinary printer paper or thin card that folds into a standard envelope.

Keeping the letter after Christmas

Take a photo or a photocopy before the letter goes in the post, and write the year on the back of the copy. Kept in order, a stack of them shows how handwriting and wishes change.

Other ways to keep the tradition going:

  • Write the letter on the same evening each year, for example the first weekend of December.
  • Pair it with a family countdown from our advent calendar ideas: one day can simply say "write to Santa".
  • If you do the Elf on the Shelf, the elf can "carry" the letter; our Elf on the Shelf ideas include a blank page the elf promises to deliver.
  • After Christmas, a child can write a thank-you note to Santa, or to the relatives who sent gifts. The thank-you card wording guide has short lines and notes that a child's drawing can go with them.
  • An older child who has stopped writing to Santa can make a coupon book for a younger sibling instead.

Questions people ask

What is Santa's address in 2026?

In the US, USPS Operation Santa uses Santa, 123 Elf Road, North Pole, 88888, with a First-Class stamp, by December 5, 2026. In Canada it is Santa Claus, North Pole, H0H 0H0, Canada, no stamp needed, by December 9, 2026. In the UK the Post Office gives Santa/Father Christmas, Santa's Grotto, Reindeerland, XM4 5HQ, by 9 December 2026.

Does Santa write back?

Canada Post says Santa responds to all letters he receives, and asks for a return address on each letter. USPS Operation Santa says there is no guarantee that a letter is uploaded, adopted or answered. The UK Post Office page asks children to include their name and address so Santa can reply.

What should a child write in a letter to Santa?

Start with 'Dear Santa', say how old they are and one thing they did this year, list two to four wishes in order, ask Santa a question, and sign with their first name. USPS asks for specific gift descriptions and requests that aren't too expensive.

Can adults write to Santa?

USPS Operation Santa says there is no age limit, and a parent, teacher or caregiver may help a younger child or older adult write the letter.

When should you mail a letter to Santa?

Before the official deadline of your country: December 5, 2026 for USPS Operation Santa and December 9, 2026 for Canada Post and the UK Post Office service. USPS started accepting letters on September 14, so earlier is better if you hope for an adopter.
