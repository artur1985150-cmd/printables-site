In short

  • Use sticker paper made for your printer type: Avery says inkjet ink never dries on laser-only sheets, and laser heat can melt inkjet sheets.
  • Print at 100% (Actual Size) with the paper type set to labels, heavyweight or cardstock, after a test on plain paper.
  • Cut by hand for a few stickers, or use Cricut Print Then Cut, which reads printed sensor marks and cuts around each design.
  • On Letter paper, Cricut's Print Then Cut area is up to 9.94 in tall or 7.44 in wide on Explore, Maker, Venture and Joy Xtra machines.
  • No sticker paper? Print on plain paper and seal the cut-out design between layers of clear packing tape.

To make stickers at home, design or download your artwork, print it on sticker paper made for your printer type at 100% (Actual Size), and cut the stickers out with scissors or a cutting machine such as a Cricut using Print Then Cut. With no sticker paper, print on plain paper and seal each cut-out between two layers of clear packing tape. Add a clear laminate on top if the stickers need to handle water.

What you need to make stickers at home

Method You need Best for
Sticker paper and scissors Printer, sticker paper for that printer, scissors or craft knife A few stickers, simple shapes
Cricut Print Then Cut Color printer, sticker paper, a Print Then Cut machine, Design Space Many stickers, detailed shapes, sticker sheets
Packing tape Plain paper, clear packing tape, parchment or wax paper, scissors No sticker paper on hand, kids' crafts

The artwork can be your own drawing, a photo, or clipart and PNG files you have the right to use. Check the licence of downloaded designs before you print them as stickers, and especially before you give them away or sell them.

How to make stickers with a printer

Sticker "paper" is any printable adhesive sheet; Avery points out it ranges from paper to vinyl. What matters most is matching it to your printer. Avery explains why: laser printers fuse toner with heat, inkjet printers spray wet ink. Inkjet ink on a laser-only sheet never soaks in, so it smears. Inkjet-only sheets in a laser printer can discolour or melt and damage the printer. Sheets that work in both say laser/inkjet or inkjet/laser on the pack.

Then:

  1. Lay out your stickers on a page the size of your sheet, usually US Letter or A4.
  2. Print a test on plain paper first. Hold it over the sticker sheet against the light to check that the layout fits.
  3. In the print dialog, set scaling to 100% or Actual Size, never Fit to Page.
  4. Set the paper type to Labels, Heavyweight or Cardstock, which Avery recommends so thicker sheets feed smoothly.
  5. Print one sheet. Inkjet sticker paper absorbs the ink so it can sink in and dry, so give it a few minutes before handling.
  6. Cut the stickers out with scissors or a craft knife. Leave a thin white border around detailed shapes; it is far easier to cut than a tight outline.

Which printer suits which sticker? Avery's advice is that inkjet printers are better for photos, while laser printers suit large batches and waterproof labels.

How to make stickers with Cricut (Print Then Cut)

Print Then Cut lets Design Space print your design with black sensor marks around it on your home printer. The machine scans those marks and cuts around each sticker. Cricut lists Joy Xtra, Explore, Maker and Venture machines as able to do it; the original Cricut Joy cannot.

  1. Calibrate the machine for Print Then Cut first (Design Space settings, Machines, Calibrate).
  2. Start a sticker project in Design Space, add your images or upload your own, and size them.
  3. Choose kiss cut (through the sticker layer only, so the stickers stay on a sheet) or die cut (through the backing too, for single stickers). Cricut's sticker flow uses kiss cut by default.
  4. Select Make and print. Cricut recommends leaving Bleed on, a small border of colour that gets trimmed off during cutting.
  5. Put the printed sheet in the top left corner of the mat, load it and cut.

Cricut's printer settings for this: portrait mode, the highest quality setting, one page per sheet, no edge (borderless) printing and no scaling or "fit to page". Cricut recommends an inkjet printer. Laser printers are not for heat-sensitive materials such as printable vinyl.

One rule catches people out. Cricut says to do the whole job, from printing to cutting, in one Design Space session on the same device. If you save the sheet as a PDF and print it later from another program, the sensor marks come out the wrong size and the cuts miss.

How big can Print Then Cut stickers be?

Space on the page is taken up by the sensor marks, so the cuttable area is smaller than the paper and not a full rectangle. Cricut's limits for the Explore, Maker, Venture and Joy Xtra machines:

Paper Maximum vertical Maximum horizontal
Letter, 8.5 x 11 in 9.94 in (25.25 cm) 7.44 in (18.9 cm)
A4, 8.3 x 11.7 in 10.62 in (26.98 cm) 7.2 in (18.3 cm)
Legal, 8.5 x 14 in (not Joy Xtra) 12.94 in (32.87 cm) 7.44 in (18.9 cm)

A design that uses the full height may not be able to use the full width at the same time. If it is too big, Design Space shows a warning icon in the Layers panel and offers Auto-Resize Image or Change Page Size. Only page sizes your machine supports appear in that list.

To see how many stickers fit, Cricut suggests making one sticker at the size you want, clicking Make, and raising the number of copies on the Prepare page until the page is full.

Which sticker paper works with Cricut?

Older machines are fussier. Cricut says the Explore Air 2 and earlier need non-reflective matte white or transparent material; glossy, reflective or dark sheets stop the sensor reading the marks. Explore 3 and newer, all Maker machines, Venture and Joy Xtra handle white, light-coloured and simply patterned materials, though non-Cricut sheets with high gloss or holographic finishes can still upset the scan.

For planner stickers, Cricut's guide notes that printable vinyl makes a thinner sticker that keeps pages from getting thick, but it tears more easily when you peel it.

How to make stickers without sticker paper (packing tape)

This method needs no special paper:

  1. Tape a sheet of parchment, wax or freezer paper to the table.
  2. Cover it with overlapping strips of clear packing tape, smoothing each one.
  3. Print or draw your designs on plain paper and cut them out.
  4. Place them face up on the tape.
  5. Cover each design with another strip of tape and smooth out bubbles.
  6. Cut around each one, leaving a narrow clear border so the edges stay sealed.

The result is a glossy sticker with tape adhesive on the back. They are good for kids' crafts and quick decorations; for anything you want to peel and restick cleanly, printable sticker paper is the better choice.

How to make waterproof stickers at home

Plain sticker paper is for dry places. For water bottles and other things that get wet, use a vinyl sheet and a clear laminate on top.

  • Cricut's waterproof sticker sets come with printable vinyl and laminate sheets. You line the laminate up with the printed sheet, peel back the top strip of its backing, and roll it on slowly while peeling, then cut.
  • Avery says its vinyl sheets are the choice for waterproof stickers and recommends laser printers for them.
  • Self-adhesive laminating sheets laid over a printed sticker sheet before cutting work on the same principle.

Where homemade stickers go next

Stickers you make at home suit planners, journals and gift wrap. For a planner kept on a tablet, the same PNG files work as digital stickers in Goodnotes. If you want artwork on a drinkware blank rather than a sticker, sublimation is a separate process with its own templates: see our sublimation tumbler wrap size chart. Spare sticker sheets also make quick work of decorating handmade bookmarks.

Before cutting a whole sheet, cut one sticker as a test; Cricut suggests the same before any bulk project.

Questions people ask

Can I make stickers with a regular printer?

Yes. Any inkjet or laser printer works as long as the sticker paper is made for that printer type, or is marked for both. Avery labels such sheets laser/inkjet or inkjet/laser on the pack and product page.

How do I make stickers without sticker paper?

Print or draw the design on plain paper, cut it out, lay it face up on a strip of clear packing tape stuck to parchment or wax paper, cover it with a second strip, then cut around it with a small clear border.

How big can I make stickers with Cricut Print Then Cut?

It depends on the machine and paper size. On Letter paper, Cricut lists up to 9.94 in vertical or 7.44 in horizontal for Explore, Maker, Venture and Joy Xtra; on A4, 10.62 in vertical or 7.2 in horizontal. The area is not a full rectangle, so a design may not reach both at once.

Why is my Cricut cutting in the wrong place on printed stickers?

Cricut says to print and cut in one Design Space session from the same device. Saving the project as a PDF and printing it outside Design Space gives wrongly sized sensor marks. Also check calibration and that your printer is set not to scale the page.

How do I make stickers waterproof at home?

Use printable vinyl with a clear laminate over it. Cricut's waterproof sticker sets pair printable vinyl with laminate sheets that you smooth on before cutting; Avery recommends laser printers for its vinyl sheets when you need waterproof stickers.
