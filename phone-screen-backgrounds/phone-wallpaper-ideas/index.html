In short

  • Plan the picture around what covers it: the clock and widgets near the top of the Lock Screen, app icons over most of the Home Screen.
  • Use a pair: a bold picture on the Lock Screen and a quieter one behind the icons. iPhone and Pixel both let you set them separately.
  • Words (quotes, verses, affirmations, names) read best in the lower middle of the Lock Screen, clear of the clock and the bottom controls.
  • Useful wallpapers carry information: a class schedule, a month view, a packing list, an emergency contact card.
  • A template is just a canvas at your phone's pixel size with the clock zone marked; we show how to make one.

The best phone wallpaper ideas are planned around what sits on top of the picture: the clock and widgets near the top of the Lock Screen, and rows of app icons on the Home Screen. Use a bold picture on the Lock Screen and a calmer one behind the icons, put words in the lower middle, and make the file at your phone's exact pixel size.

Below are ideas sorted by what they do, then a simple way to build your own template so every new design fits.

Start with a pair, not one picture

Both iPhone and Pixel let the two screens show different pictures. Apple's help page has two buttons after you pick a photo: Set as Wallpaper Pair, which puts it on both, and Customize Home Screen, which lets you give the Home Screen its own version. On a Pixel, the wallpaper picker asks whether the photo goes on the Lock screen or the Home screen.

That opens up pairs like these:

  • A photo on the Lock Screen and the same photo cropped to a patch of sky or wall for the Home Screen.
  • A busy pattern on the Lock Screen and one of its colours as a solid behind the icons.
  • A quote card on the Lock Screen and a plain texture in the same tone on the Home Screen.
  • A dark illustration on the Lock Screen and a darker, blurred copy on the Home Screen.

You look at the Lock Screen picture; on the Home Screen you are hunting for an app, and a quiet background makes the icons easier to find.

Phone wallpaper ideas that carry information

A wallpaper is the screen you see most often, which makes it a good place for things you check every day.

People search for "phone wallpaper class schedule" for a reason: it is quicker than opening an app. Keep the type large and the lines few. A Lock Screen is small; at about 2.6 x 5.7 inches on an iPhone 17 (1206 x 2622 pixels at 460 per inch, from Apple's spec page), anything below a readable size is decoration.

An emergency card on a Lock Screen is visible to anyone who picks up the phone, so put only what you want a stranger to read.

Quote, verse and affirmation wallpapers

Affirmations, Bible verses, quotes and names all turn up in what people type when they look for wallpapers. Words are also the easiest to get wrong, because the clock wants the same space.

  • Place the words in the lower middle of the Lock Screen, under the clock and widgets and above the bottom controls.
  • Keep it short: one line of up to about eight words, or two short lines.
  • Use a plain or softly blurred background behind the words so they read in a glance.
  • Set the Home Screen to the background alone, with no text; icons would cover it anyway.
  • Preview before you apply. Apple's Customize screen and the Pixel's Preview step both show the real layout.

On iPhone you can also change how the time looks in Customize, which helps when the font of your quote clashes with the clock.

Photo wallpaper ideas

Your own photos make the most personal wallpapers. A few ways to use them well:

  • Crop to a part of the photo with an empty area at the top, like sky over a landscape, so the clock sits on something calm.
  • Use a vertical photo. A landscape photo has to be enlarged and cropped hard to fill a tall screen.
  • Make a collage of three or four photos stacked vertically, with the top strip kept plain for the clock.
  • Let the phone rotate them. Apple's Photo Shuffle changes the picture on tap, on lock, hourly or daily, from categories such as People, Nature or Cities, or from one album.
  • On iPhone 12 or later, the Spatial Scene tab in Photos turns suitable photos into a 3D wallpaper, according to Apple's help page.

Pixel's Daily wallpaper also changes the picture every day, but Google notes it does not work with your own photos or with live wallpapers.

Pattern, colour and seasonal ideas

Patterns and solids are kind to icons and clocks. The searches show the range: minimalist, gradient, black, white, boho, floral, stars, Y2K.

Seasonal sets are a good reason to change the picture: autumn leaves, Halloween, a winter pattern, Christmas, spring flowers, a summer beach. Make each season as a pair, so the Home Screen version is the pattern's quietest colour.

On a Pixel, colour matters twice. Google's help page says you can pick a preset colour palette that corresponds with your wallpaper, so a wallpaper with two or three clear colours gives the system a clean palette to work from.

How to make a phone wallpaper template

A template is a reusable canvas that already knows your screen. You make it once per phone.

  1. Find your phone's resolution on the maker's spec page or in our phone wallpaper size table. For an iPhone 17 it is 1206 x 2622; for a Pixel 11, 1080 x 2424.
  2. Make a test image at that size: a plain colour with a grid of lines every 100 pixels and the numbers written along the edges.
  3. Set it as both Lock Screen and Home Screen wallpaper.
  4. Take a screenshot of each screen and check its pixel size in your photo app. If it matches your canvas, it lines up exactly.
  5. In your design app, start a canvas at the same size and place both screenshots on it as locked, half-transparent guide layers.
  6. Read off where your clock, widgets, dock and icon rows fall, and draw simple boxes over them on a separate guide layer.
  7. Delete the screenshots and save the file with only the guide boxes. Hide the guides when you export each new design.

Do it once per phone you design for. If you make wallpapers for several phones at once, our size page explains the single 1440 x 3240 size that fills all the phones in our table.

Before you set it: a short checklist

  • The file is your screen's exact pixel size, or larger in the same shape.
  • Nothing important sits under the clock or the bottom controls.
  • Text is in the lower middle and large enough to read without zooming.
  • The Home Screen version is calmer than the Lock Screen one.
  • You looked at it in the phone's preview before applying.

Questions people ask

What is a good idea for a phone wallpaper?

Pick one picture for the Lock Screen and a calmer version for the Home Screen: a photo and the same photo blurred or cropped to sky, a pattern and its plainest colour, a quote card and a matching solid. The pair keeps the clock and the app icons easy to read.

How do I make a phone wallpaper template?

Start a canvas at your phone's exact pixel size, such as 1206 x 2622 for an iPhone 17. Set a test wallpaper with a grid on it, take a screenshot of the Lock Screen and Home Screen, and lay the screenshots over the canvas as a guide layer. That shows where the clock, widgets and icons fall on your phone.

Where should text go on a phone wallpaper?

Below the clock and above the bottom controls on the Lock Screen, roughly the lower middle. On the Home Screen almost every spot is under an icon, so words there are hard to read.

Can I have different wallpapers on the lock screen and home screen?

Yes. On iPhone, add the wallpaper and choose Customize Home Screen instead of Set as Wallpaper Pair. On a Pixel, the wallpaper picker lets you pick Lock screen or Home screen before you apply it.

Can my wallpaper change by itself?

On iPhone, Photo Shuffle rotates photos from chosen categories or an album on tap, on lock, hourly or daily. On a Pixel, Daily wallpaper changes the picture each day from a category, but Google says it does not work with your own photos.
