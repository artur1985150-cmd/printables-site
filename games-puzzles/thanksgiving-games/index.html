When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving 2026 is on Thursday, November 26. US federal law (5 U.S.C. § 6103) sets Thanksgiving Day as "the fourth Thursday in November", and the Office of Personnel Management lists Thursday, November 26 as Thanksgiving Day in its 2026 federal holiday schedule.

That gives you a little under two weeks from the day this page goes up to print and cut any games.

Thanksgiving games to play before dinner

Before the meal, people stand around the kitchen and children get restless. This is the time for games that move.

  • Turkey bowling: a small ball rolled at plastic cups or empty bottles set up as pins.
  • Charades: act out Thanksgiving foods, parade balloons or family memories.
  • Thanksgiving scavenger hunt: paper turkeys hidden around the house or yard, a list of things to find.
  • Minute-to-win-it games: Play Party Plan has a long list, such as Football Flick and Feather Float.
  • Pumpkin roll: roll a small pumpkin to a line across the lawn.
  • Two truths and a lie, for adults standing in the kitchen.

Party & Beyond suggests starting games some time after guests arrive and before dinner, and splitting a large crowd into teams.

Games to play at the Thanksgiving table

At the table, games need to be quiet, quick and played sitting down.

  • Gratitude cards: each person draws a card ("something that made you laugh this year") and answers.
  • Thanksgiving this or that: pie or cake, stuffing or mashed potatoes.
  • Would you rather, Thanksgiving edition.
  • Placemat games: printed placemats with a word search, maze or tic-tac-toe for children.
  • Guess the year: pass round old family photos and guess when each was taken.
  • Name that dish: a blindfolded taste test of the sides.

Thanksgiving games for kids

Children who finish eating first need something to do while adults talk.

  • Roll a turkey: roll a die and draw the part that matches the number (body, head, feathers) until the turkey is done.
  • Thanksgiving I spy sheets.
  • Word searches and mazes.
  • Thanksgiving bingo with pictures for children who don't read yet.
  • A turkey hunt: hide paper turkeys and count who finds most.
  • Colouring pages or a dot marker activities sheet for the youngest.

Our guide to party games for kids has more games sorted by age and space, plus the foods to avoid giving small children during eating games.

Thanksgiving games for adults

Adults after dinner want to sit and talk, maybe compete a little.

  • Thanksgiving Scattergories: a letter and a list of categories (side dish, something orange, a parade balloon).
  • Trivia in teams, from a printed sheet or read aloud.
  • Family feud: survey the family before the day and guess the top answers.
  • Thanksgiving Pictionary on a pad or a whiteboard.
  • Two truths and a lie.
  • Thanksgiving word game or matching game from a printable.

Play Party Plan puts its trivia, word, Scattergories and matching games in an adult section and says they suit teens and up.

Printable Thanksgiving games

Printing a few games the week before means nobody has to invent rules on the day.

What to print:

  1. Bingo cards, one per player, plus calling cards.
  2. Trivia sheets and an answer key.
  3. Roll-a-turkey sheets and a die for the kids' table.
  4. I spy pages and word searches.
  5. Gratitude prompt cards for a jar on the table.
  6. Scattergories lists.
  7. Placemats with games for the children's seats.

Write the rules on the front of each game's envelope, so whoever runs it doesn't need to find you.

Thanksgiving games with no supplies

If nothing got printed, these need only the people at the table:

  • Charades, with a phone timer.
  • Twenty questions: one person thinks of a Thanksgiving food or a family member, the rest ask yes or no questions.
  • The alphabet game: go round the table naming something you're thankful for, each starting with the next letter.
  • Two truths and a lie.
  • Story chain: each person adds one sentence to a story that starts "The turkey escaped..."
  • Would you rather, made up on the spot.

For a big family group, split into teams by table or by generation, keep score on a napkin, and let the youngest player on each team go first.

Keeping food safe when games run long

Long after-dinner games are when the turkey sits out on the counter. The CDC says to refrigerate perishable food, including cooked leftovers, within 2 hours, or within 1 hour if it is exposed to temperatures above 90°F. It explains that bacteria can multiply rapidly in the "Danger Zone" between 40°F and 140°F.

Before the second round of games starts, have someone put the leftovers away. Then bring out pie.

Questions people ask

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving 2026 falls on Thursday, November 26. US law sets Thanksgiving Day as the fourth Thursday in November, and the Office of Personnel Management lists November 26 as the federal holiday for 2026.

What games can the whole family play on Thanksgiving?

Charades, Thanksgiving bingo, Pictionary, a gratitude game and turkey bowling with a small ball and paper-cup pins all work across ages. Pair younger children with an adult for anything that needs reading or scoring.

What are good Thanksgiving games for adults?

Thanksgiving Scattergories, trivia in teams, a family-feud style survey game, two truths and a lie, and a blindfolded taste test of the side dishes. They suit a table of adults after dinner, when nobody wants to run around.

What Thanksgiving games are good for kids?

Roll-a-turkey dice drawing, Thanksgiving I spy sheets, word searches, a hidden-turkey scavenger hunt and printed placemats with games on them. Printables keep children busy at the table while adults finish eating.

Should you play games before or after Thanksgiving dinner?

Both work. Play Party Plan says its games suit before or after the feast. Active games fit better before dinner while people wait for the turkey, and calmer table games after. If you play long after dinner, refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.
