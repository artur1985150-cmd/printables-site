How to run a Christmas trivia game

A trivia round works for six people or sixty, as long as everyone can hear the host. Icebreaker Ideas suggests pairs or teams of three to five, and solo play for a small group.

  1. Split guests into teams of three to five. Mix ages, so each team has someone who knows the old films and someone who knows the new songs.
  2. Give each team a pen and an answer sheet numbered for the round.
  3. Read each question twice. Don't reveal answers until the round ends.
  4. Swap sheets between teams for marking, then read the answers out.
  5. Keep a running score on a big sheet of paper where everyone can see it.

Christmas music trivia questions

  1. In what language was "Silent Night" first written? Answer: German. Its original title is "Stille Nacht, heilige Nacht".
  2. In which country was "Silent Night" composed in 1818? Answer: Austria, in Oberndorf bei Salzburg.
  3. What was "Jingle Bells" called when it was first published in 1857? Answer: "The One Horse Open Sleigh".
  4. Who wrote "White Christmas", and for which 1942 film? Answer: Irving Berlin, for "Holiday Inn".
  5. Whose recording of "White Christmas" is the best-selling single in the world? Answer: Bing Crosby's.
  6. In "The Twelve Days of Christmas", what does the true love send on the fifth day? Answer: Five gold rings.
  7. Counting every repeat in the song, how many gifts has the true love sent by the twelfth day? Answer: 364.
  8. Who recorded "Feliz Navidad" in 1970? Answer: José Feliciano.
  9. Which act released "Last Christmas" in 1984? Answer: Wham!
  10. In what year did Mariah Carey release "All I Want for Christmas Is You"? Answer: 1994.
  11. Which singer released "Jingle Bell Rock" in 1957? Answer: Bobby Helms.
  12. The melody of "Deck the Halls" comes from a carol of which country? Answer: Wales. The tune belongs to the Welsh carol "Nos Galan".
  13. What name did the 1984 charity group behind "Do They Know It's Christmas?" go by? Answer: Band Aid.

Christmas movie trivia questions

  1. In "A Christmas Carol", what is the name of Scrooge's dead business partner? Answer: Jacob Marley.
  2. What is the name of Scrooge's underpaid clerk? Answer: Bob Cratchit.
  3. In what year did Charles Dickens publish "A Christmas Carol"? Answer: 1843.
  4. In "Home Alone", where is the McCallister family flying for Christmas? Answer: Paris.
  5. What are the names of the two burglars in "Home Alone"? Answer: Harry and Marv, the "Wet Bandits".
  6. In "Elf", which city does Buddy travel to in search of his father? Answer: New York City.
  7. In "It's a Wonderful Life", what is the name of George Bailey's town? Answer: Bedford Falls.
  8. What is the name of George Bailey's guardian angel? Answer: Clarence Odbody.
  9. In the 1947 film "Miracle on 34th Street", which store's parade needs a new Santa? Answer: Macy's, for its Thanksgiving Day Parade.
  10. What is the name of the Grinch's dog? Answer: Max.
  11. By how many sizes does the Grinch's heart grow at the end of the story? Answer: Three.
  12. Which composer wrote the music for the ballet "The Nutcracker", first staged in 1892? Answer: Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky.

Bonus for film fans: "The Polar Express" started as a 1985 picture book by Chris Van Allsburg; the 2004 film starred Tom Hanks. "A Charlie Brown Christmas" first aired in 1965. Use these as extra questions if a round runs short.

Santa Claus trivia questions

  1. How many reindeer pull the sleigh in "A Visit from St. Nicholas" ('Twas the Night Before Christmas)? Answer: Eight.
  2. In what year was that poem first published? Answer: 1823, anonymously, in the Troy Sentinel.
  3. In that first printing, what were Donder and Blitzen called? Answer: Dunder and Blixem.
  4. Saint Nicholas, whose story led to Santa Claus, was bishop of which city? Answer: Myra.
  5. Who created Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer, and for which department store? Answer: Robert L. May, for Montgomery Ward, in 1939.
  6. Who turned Rudolph's story into the famous song? Answer: Johnny Marks, May's brother-in-law.
  7. Which North American military command runs the Christmas Eve Santa tracker that began in 1955? Answer: NORAD, the North American Aerospace Defense Command.
  8. A newspaper ad from which store started NORAD's Santa tracking? Answer: Sears.
  9. Haddon Sundblom painted Santa Claus for which company's ads, starting in 1931? Answer: The Coca-Cola Company.
  10. In Italy, which witch-like old woman brings gifts on the night of 5 January? Answer: La Befana.
  11. What is Santa called in Finland? The name means "Christmas goat". Answer: Joulupukki.

Christmas traditions trivia questions

  1. Which fast-food chain is a Christmas custom in Japan, thanks to an ad campaign in the 1970s? Answer: KFC.
  2. Since 1947, which city has sent the Christmas tree that stands in London's Trafalgar Square? Answer: Oslo.
  3. The Rockefeller Center Christmas tree has been a national tradition every year since which year? Answer: 1933. (The first, smaller tree went up in 1931.)
  4. Which newspaper's 1848 picture of the royal family's tree at Windsor Castle boosted the Christmas tree trend in Britain? Answer: The Illustrated London News.
  5. The poinsettia is named after Joel Roberts Poinsett. What was his job? Answer: He was the first United States minister to Mexico.
  6. Who commissioned the first commercial Christmas card in 1843? Answer: Sir Henry Cole. John Callcott Horsley designed it.
  7. In what year was Christmas declared a United States federal holiday? Answer: 1870.
  8. In 1659, celebrating Christmas was outlawed in which American city? Answer: Boston. Until 1681, the fine was five shillings.
  9. On what date is Boxing Day? Answer: 26 December.
  10. Which horned figure from Alpine folklore visits with Saint Nicholas on the night of 5 December? Answer: Krampus.

Christmas food trivia questions

  1. What do the French call a Yule log cake? Answer: Bûche de Noël.
  2. Panettone comes from which Italian city? Answer: Milan.
  3. Stollen from which German city was first mentioned in an official document in 1474? Answer: Dresden.
  4. What was eggnog historically called when it contained alcohol? Answer: Milk punch, or egg milk punch.
  5. What is the filling of a mince pie called? Answer: Mincemeat.
  6. Which animal fat is a main ingredient of a traditional Christmas pudding? Answer: Suet.

Easy Christmas trivia for kids

Children who know the classic films and songs can play the main game with an adult on their team. For a kids-only round, these questions from above have short, familiar answers:

  • 6: five gold rings
  • 19: New York City
  • 23: Max
  • 24: three sizes
  • 26: eight reindeer
  • 17: Paris
  • 45: 26 December

Add a drawing answer for the youngest: "Draw what the true love sends on the first day" (a partridge in a pear tree). Younger children can also answer out loud while an adult on their team writes.

Christmas trivia answers people often get wrong

Some questions turn up on many trivia lists with an answer stated as fact, when reference pages call it a legend or say the question is disputed. We left these out:

  • Why candy canes are shaped like a hook. Wikipedia's candy cane article describes the Cologne choirmaster version as "a common story" and does not confirm it.
  • Who wrote 'Twas the Night Before Christmas. Clement Clarke Moore claimed it in 1837, but the Wikipedia article also notes the case made for Henry Livingston Jr. We ask about the year and the reindeer instead.
  • Exact Christmas card or tree counts for a whole country. Numbers like these change every year and are hard to trace to an original count.

Questions people ask

What are some good Christmas trivia questions?

Questions with one clear answer: what 'Jingle Bells' was first called (The One Horse Open Sleigh), what the Grinch's dog is called (Max), which city sends London's Trafalgar Square tree (Oslo), and what the French call a Yule log cake (bûche de Noël).

How many questions should a Christmas trivia game have?

For a party, five rounds of six to thirteen questions, as on this page, is plenty. Cut a round if the group is young or the evening is short, and time your own first round to see how long the rest will take.

What is a hard Christmas trivia question?

Try: 'Counting every repeat, how many gifts does the true love give in The Twelve Days of Christmas?' The answer is 364. Or: 'What were Donder and Blitzen called in the 1823 printing of A Visit from St. Nicholas?' Dunder and Blixem.

What are easy Christmas trivia questions for kids?

How many reindeer pull Santa's sleigh in 'Twas the Night Before Christmas (eight), what the Grinch's dog is called (Max), where Buddy the Elf travels to (New York City), and what the boy in Home Alone is called (Kevin McCallister).
